Portal Live Chat

Secure, authenticated customer chat right inside your Odoo Customer Portal. Seamlessly connect logged-in clients with agents in Discuss — with real contact identification, continuous chat history, and attachment sharing.

Why Portal Live Chat?

Transform anonymous visitor chaos into structured, relationship-building conversations.

Standard Live Chat Limitations

  • Operators see confusing anonymous tags: "Visitor #1024".
  • Refreshing or logging in spawns duplicate, fragmented sessions.
  • Public bots and anonymous traffic overwhelm support agents.
  • Customers cannot easily attach contracts, PDFs, or receipts in CE.
  • Usually demands a heavy dependency on the Odoo Website builder.

With Portal Live Chat

  • Instant Identification: Operators see the verified client name & email.
  • Persistent History: The client resumes their ongoing conversation anytime.
  • Privacy Protection: Gate the chat widget strictly to authenticated portal users.
  • Document Sharing: Full composer file attachment support out-of-the-box.
  • Zero Website Bloat: Works seamlessly on pure Portal setups.

Key Features & Capabilities

Engineered for high-performing client service teams and B2B portal workflows.

Automatic Contact Linking

Whenever a client initiates or resumes a chat, their Odoo Contact (`res.partner`) is linked directly to the conversation channel in Discuss.

Continuous Session History

Clients logging into the portal reconnect to their existing conversation instead of opening new orphan sessions. Past context is always preserved.

File & Document Uploads

Enables portal clients to share invoices, screenshots, error logs, and PDF agreements directly through the live chat widget.

Flexible Visibility Modes

Choose between strict Portal Users Only mode or All Visitors with Auto-Identification from General Settings.

Anti-Spam Server Gates

Restricts `/im_livechat/init` and `/im_livechat/get_session` at the controller level so bots cannot bypass UI restrictions.

Native Discuss Integration

Agents work comfortably in their standard Odoo Discuss interface. Incoming portal chats are automatically labeled with the client's name.

Simple 3-Step Setup

Get up and running in less than 2 minutes without writing a single line of code.

1
Create Channel

Go to Live Chat → Channels, create your channel and assign operators/employees.

2
Select in Settings

Navigate to Settings → General Settings → Portal Live Chat and pick your active channel.

3
Client Logs In

Clients log in to /my. The live chat bubble appears automatically and routes directly to agents in Discuss.
