Customer Live Chat

Secure, authenticated customer live chat right inside your Odoo Customer Portal. Connect logged-in portal clients with operators in Discuss — featuring instant client identification, persistent conversation history, and out-of-the-box file sharing.

Real Client Names in Discuss Continuous Chat History PDF & Document Uploads Anti-Spam Portal Gates Zero Website Builder Dependency

Why Customer Live Chat?

Transform anonymous visitor chaos into structured, relationship-building conversations.

Standard Live Chat Limitations

  • Operators see confusing anonymous tags like "Visitor #1024".
  • Refreshing or logging in creates duplicate, fragmented sessions.
  • Anonymous public traffic and bots overwhelm support agents.
  • Customers cannot easily attach invoices, PDFs, or receipts.
  • Unnecessarily forces an installation of the heavy Website module.

With Customer Live Chat

  • Instant Identification: Operators see verified client name & email.
  • Continuous History: Returning customers resume existing conversations.
  • Privacy Gate: Restrict chat visibility strictly to logged-in portal users.
  • Document Sharing: Full composer file attachment support enabled.
  • Lightweight & Fast: Operates purely on Portal & Live Chat.

Key Features & Capabilities

Engineered for high-performing client service teams and B2B portal workflows.

Automatic Contact Linking

Whenever a client initiates or resumes a chat, their Odoo Contact (res.partner) is linked directly to Discuss.

Continuous Session History

Clients logging into the portal reconnect to their existing conversation instead of opening new orphan sessions.

File & Document Uploads

Enables portal clients to share invoices, screenshots, error logs, and PDF agreements directly in chat.

Flexible Visibility Modes

Choose between strict Portal Users Only mode or All Visitors with Auto-Identification from General Settings.

Anti-Spam Server Gates

Restricts /im_livechat/init and /im_livechat/get_session at the controller level against bot requests.

Native Discuss Integration

Agents work comfortably in standard Odoo Discuss. Incoming portal chats are cleanly labeled with client names.

Simple 3-Step Setup

Get up and running in less than 2 minutes without writing code.

1
Create Channel

Go to Live Chat → Channels, create your channel and assign operators/employees.

2
Select in Settings

Navigate to Settings → General Settings → Portal Live Chat and pick your active channel.

3
Client Logs In

Clients log in to /my. The live chat bubble appears automatically and routes directly to Discuss.
